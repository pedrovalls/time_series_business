Seven lectures covering the theory and practice of time series econometrics, from ARMA and unit roots through VAR, structural breaks, GARCH, and modern model-selection methods. Each lecture comes with a set of slides, lecture notes, and an applied tutorial using publicly available data from finance or marketing.
